# Data

`student-mat.csv` and `student-por.csv` are the Student Performance dataset from the
UCI Machine Learning Repository, unchanged. `student.txt` is the authors' description
of every column.

P. Cortez and A. Silva. "Using Data Mining to Predict Secondary School Student
Performance." In A. Brito and J. Teixeira (eds.), Proceedings of the 5th FUture BUsiness
TEChnology Conference (FUBUTEC 2008), pp. 5-12, Porto, Portugal, April 2008.
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/320/student+performance
